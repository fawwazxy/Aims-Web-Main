Our Leader

Tuan Haji Aminudin bin Mat Yusof

Executive Chairman

Tuan Haji Aminudin bin Mat Yusof is the founder and appointed as Executive Chairman of AIMS-GLOBAL. He is an entrepreneur with extensive interest and experience in oil & gas, maritime, agriculture, construction, management solutions & services and education.

He involved and succeeded in acquisition and privatization of the Mara Shipyard & Engineering Terengganu Sdn. Bhd. in 1997 and Permint Engineering Sdn. Bhd. in 2002.

Other than AIMS-GLOBAL, Tuan Haji Aminudin also holds directorships in MSET Groups (MSET Shipbuilding Corporation Sdn. Bhd. , MSET Inflatable Corporation Sdn. Bhd. and MSET Offshore Corporation Sdn. Bhd.) , Ultimate Double Sdn. Bhd., SMIPS Education Sdn. Bhd., MCM Corporate Services Sdn. Bhd., Yayasan Sulaiman Al-Haj and Iman Permai Sdn. Bhd.



Captain Mohd Tarmizi bin Mejam

Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

Captain Mohd Tarmizi bin Mejam was appointed as Group Chief Executive Officer (Group CEO) of AIMS-GLOBAL since December 2012 and Executive Director of AIMS-GLOBAL. He also holds directorship in MSET Group. He has extensive experiences in the international maritime industry for over seventeen years. He also qualified as a Master Mariner with a Masters in Foreign-Going Unlimited. He joined AIMS-GLOBAL since May 2009.

Before joining the AIMS-GLOBAL , he served as Master/Captain in Malaysia International Shipping Corporation (“MISC”) Berhad, who were responsible to handle full in command of the vessel. He was also entrusted by the owners for the safety and efficient day to day running of the vessel in complying with all industry standard, International Rules, Regulation and Convention.
