Get to know about our company

AIMS-GLOBAL HOLDINGS SDN. BHD. (“AGHSB”) is an investment holdings company with the Group of Companies, providing services in:
• Ship Owner & Ship Management
• Trading & Maintenance Services
• Port & Agency Management
• Offshore Construction & Maintenance Services
• Onshore Fabrication & Maintenance Services
• Ship Repair & Ship Building

AIMS-GLOBAL HOLDINGS SDN. BHD. (“AGHSB”) commenced their business in 2009. At that time, one of the subsidiaries known as AIMS-GLOBAL ENGINEERING SDN. BHD. (“AGESB”) where they started their operation as a ship operator for Offshore Support Vessel (“OSV”). Today, “AGHSB” and the group of companies also known as AIMS-GLOBAL, have emerged into reputable integrated marine offshore services provider operating in the South East Asia.
